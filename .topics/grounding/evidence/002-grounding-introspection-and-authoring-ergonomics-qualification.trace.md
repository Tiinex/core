## Preservation And Fidelity

- Preservation State: delegated Core mechanics are implemented and locally qualified; exact semantic gaps remain explicit and unfilled
- Known Losses: none in the delegated Core source surface; process applicability and wider participant semantics remain unresolved by design rather than being approximated
- Fidelity Notes: provenance is attached to existing facts rather than introducing a new semantic lifecycle taxonomy; exact Handoff/Role/Workspace authority boundaries remain controlling
