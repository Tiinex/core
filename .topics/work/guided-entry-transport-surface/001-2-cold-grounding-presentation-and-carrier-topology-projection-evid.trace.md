# Cold Grounding Presentation And Carrier Topology Projection Evidence

## Supported Claim Or Question

- Supported Claim Or Question: whether compact Guided Entry transport can remain reference-first while making the cold recipient explicitly apply participating Role presentation preferences and ground carrier branching/Major semantics without duplicating semantic content into transport prose.
- Evidence Role: bounded implementation and regression evidence for the Guided Entry reference-first transport surface and portable cold-grounding bootstrap.
- Target Artifact: Guided Entry Reference First Transport Surface.
- Review Context: the cold Anchor correctly preserved authority boundaries but initially failed to apply Sigma's qualified TL;DR/pattern-first presentation preference and explained carrier lineage too linearly until Sigma surfaced the intended branching model.

## Provenance

- Known Source: exact pointerless Workspace carrier `tiinex-027-1.handoff-package.zip`, exact carried Resume Entry and Sigma Role material, and the current local Core candidate derived from that carrier.
- Preservation Basis: Core projection/bootstrap text and tests only; compact transport remains reference-first and no Role preference prose is duplicated into the transport payload.
- Provenance Limits: this Evidence does not make Session Role or Participant selection semantic authority and does not select a current Task or Process applicability.

## Evidence Material

- Material: cold-recipient presentation preference projection plus bootstrap grounding for carrier branching and Major checkpoints.
- Material Kind: implementation and regression evidence.
- Participant Presentation: when the selected purpose Entry declares participating-Role preference sources, Guided Entry transport now instructs the cold recipient to qualify the exact selected Role materials and apply declared presentation/communication preferences to human-facing presentation only.
- Non-Authority Boundary: presentation preferences must never become holder, participant, recipient, work-transfer, acceptance, or mutation authority.
- Compactness Boundary: the transport intentionally does not embed strings such as `TL;DR`; the exact Role reference remains the source of presentation semantics.
- Workspace Representation Reminder: bootstrap now separates Workspace identity from Workspace Representation coverage and forbids Workspace-wide hygiene/Normalize/rebase conclusions from bounded, partial, or unknown representations.
- Carrier Topology: bootstrap now describes the numeric carrier suffix as a branching carrier-continuity tree rather than a turn counter.
- Parallel Return Rule: ordinary return children of parallel qualified Handoff routes use exact qualified Handoff Pointer order as dense local sibling ordinals `1..N`; consolidation occupies sibling `N+1`.
- Major Boundary: a new carrier Major is an explicit stable checkpoint/re-anchor requiring a qualified Major Reason; a new chat, Handoff, return, retry, or long-looking filename does not itself cause a Major bump.
- Separation Preserved: carrier prefix/dimension and outer filenames remain transport/recovery lineage and never substitute for semantic `Parent`, artifact lineage, Role assignment, participation, or Handoff authority.
