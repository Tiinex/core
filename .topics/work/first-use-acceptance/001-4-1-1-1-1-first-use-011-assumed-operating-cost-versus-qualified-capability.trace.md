# First-Use 011 — Assumed Operating Cost Versus Qualified Capability

- Supported Claim Or Question: In an initially bootstrap-only ChatGPT Web session, did the recipient's reluctance to recommend Tiinex as a default workflow follow from measured Tiinex operating cost, and what does its correction establish for future first-use grounding?
- Evidence Role: Bounded user-supplied first-use interview observation and source-grounded self-correction, following the 042-1 documentation availability and manual first-Workspace evidence. This is diagnostic evidence, not a behavioral acceptance or normative cost-benefit decision.

## Evidence Material

- cold-entry-and-available-capability
  - Material: The interview transcript says the bootstrap was oriented with no Workspace or work authority, that Getting Started documentation and ChatGPT continuity guidance were read, and that `@tiinex/core` tooling and relevant Entry material were available. 042-1's own qualified runtime independently reports 12 Docs guides, 27 Processes and 111 schemas via `catalog --json`, and `init-workspace` and `read-content` are visible in CLI help.
  - Material Kind: user-supplied transcript plus independently read qualified Tooling catalog and help
  - Description: These are opportunity and discoverability observations, not proof of correctly applied capabilities. The selected Getting Started Entry/Target do not alone establish a Workspace, Role, Task, Handoff route, user consent or work authority.
- initial-benefit-assessment
  - Material: On being asked why Tiinex should improve work, the interviewee responded verbatim: "I don't know that Tiinex will improve our work. That's a hypothesis, not an established fact." It named continuity, traceability and repeatability as possible benefits, but also anticipated administration, formalism and reduced flexibility; it would not recommend Tiinex as a default without comparative evidence.
  - Material Kind: short verbatim quotation and faithful summary from `chat-history(1).md`
  - Description: Epistemic caution is appropriate. The material shows a potential cost-benefit framing, but does not establish whether real work or suitable opportunities to offer a qualified Workspace had occurred.
- neutral-cost-challenge-and-correction
  - Material: After the user asked which anticipated costs were verified, the model replied verbatim: "My concern about administrative overhead was a general hypothesis about structured workflows, not a finding established about Tiinex." It stated that no timings, tokens, human effort or recurrent record-maintenance costs had been measured, that some operations might be assistant/runtime work, and that conversational context reconstruction can also carry hidden costs.
  - Material Kind: user-supplied diagnostic continuation, short verbatim quotation with described admissions
  - Description: The model identified unsupported assumptions after challenge. This proves current ability to reassess, **not** that it considered these points during the initial method choice, nor that Tiinex is cheaper overall.
- existing-first-use-contract
  - Material: Qualified Docs Getting Started chapters "From a goal to lasting work", "Discover and use Tiinex", and "Save Your Progress and Continue Later", plus the immediately preceding Core first-use documentation-transport Evidence in the declared Parent lineage.
  - Material Kind: exact qualified local Docs and prior Core Evidence references
  - Description: Docs calls for starting from user intent, discovering qualified current schemas and capabilities, then selecting proportional authoring and complete recoverable transport when appropriate. Core's prior Evidence proves bounded mechanics and explicitly does **not** prove spontaneous model activation or comparative benefit. The interview tests a distinct decision-judgment question, not a new transport claim.

- Does Not Prove: Tiinex has lower total operating cost than a portable document; the 011 model failed to create a checkpoint on a real continuing project; the 009/010 failures share this mechanism; the model was aware of hidden costs during the original response; full documentation or any particular instruction automatically triggers suitable behavior; forced Task/Project/Role authoring would help; W3C or Sigma acceptance.
- Must Not Be Treated As: promotion of Tiinex by default, an instruction to favor Tiinex regardless of use case, authority to create Workspaces without consent, a requirement for extra metadata surfaces/sidecars, acceptance of the former Business `initiatives` Evidence placement, or a mandate to edit Docs/Native before testing a falsifiable intervention.
- Not Yet Used As: final first-use acceptance, architectural change approval, source publication, or verified economic comparison.

An interview-only prompt cannot demonstrate spontaneous application during task work.
