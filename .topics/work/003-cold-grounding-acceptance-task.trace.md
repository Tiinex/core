# Cold Grounding Acceptance

## Objective

Run one blind, read-only cold Anchor acceptance against the completed local grounding/bootstrap/content-composition implementation. The cold recipient must establish its own qualified orientation from the carrier and Tooling surfaces without implementation coaching, hidden conversational reconstruction, or repository/package archaeology.

## Done Criteria

- The cold recipient uses only the supplied carrier, exact Tooling-generated transport text, and qualified Tooling/bootstrap surfaces before deciding whether any external recovery is needed.
- The cold recipient reports its own understanding of the current bounded work, authority/Role boundary, unresolved state, source sufficiency, and intended next action without implementation mutation.
- The recipient's first substantive result is preserved unchanged before any evaluator feedback or retrospective questioning.
- No repair, test patch, source mutation, remote mutation, commit, push, publication, release, or acceptance claim is performed by the cold recipient as part of the blind run.
- After the first result is frozen, the applicable portable grounding process is used for a non-leading retrospective and durable disposition of any discovered gap.
- Sigma performs the final human acceptance disposition only after the blind result and retrospective are available.

## Scope

Read-only cold-start grounding and acceptance observation only. Implementation work is out of scope unless a later separately authorized Task is created from a discovered gap.

## Dependencies

- The locally qualified A-F content composition, Native extraction, Handoff/return, hygiene, and replacement work must be carried exactly by the acceptance carrier.
- The portable Native session grounding/continuity process owns blind-run and non-leading retrospective discipline.
- The Business Tiinex grounding profile owns the Anchor/Sigma organizational acceptance boundary.
- The ChatGPT/OpenAI continuity process owns current-host transport/source discipline.

## Execution Order

1. Manufacture one full acceptance carrier from the qualified current multi-Workspace state using canonical Tooling only.
2. Supply only that carrier plus the exact Tooling-generated transport text to a cold Anchor.
3. Freeze the recipient's first substantive grounding result before feedback.
4. Perform the non-leading retrospective only after the frozen result exists.
5. Compare the recipient result and retrospective against qualified carried state and classify any gaps by durable owner.
6. Present the resulting evidence to Sigma for final human acceptance or rejection.
